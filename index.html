How is your mental health?

Over 1 in 5 U.S. adults (about 23% to 23.4%) experience a mental illness each year.

Approximately 1 in 20 U.S. adults live with a serious mental illness.

Over 1 in 7 youth (ages 6–17) experience a mental health disorder.

Half of all mental health disorders start by age 14, and three-quarters begin by age 24.
